Help

Welcome to the help page for Join, your guide to using our kanban project management tool. Here, we'll provide an overview of what Join is, how it can benefit you, and how to use it.

What is Join?

Join is a kanban-based project management tool designed and built by a group of dedicated students as part of their web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie.

Kanban, a Japanese term meaning "billboard", is a highly effective method to visualize work, limit work-in-progress, and maximize efficiency (or flow). Join leverages the principles of kanban to help users manage their tasks and projects in an intuitive, visual interface.

It is important to note that Join is designed as an educational exercise and is not intended for extensive business usage. While we strive to ensure the best possible user experience, we cannot guarantee consistent availability, reliability, accuracy, or other aspects of quality regarding Join.

How to use it

Here is a step-by-step guide on how to use Join:

  1. Exploring the Board
    The board contains four default lists: "To do", "In progress", "Await feedback" and "Done". Each represents a stage in your task's workflow.
  2. Creating Contacts
    Go to the "Contacts" section and click "Add new contact". Fill in a name, email, and phone number, then confirm with "Create contact". These contacts can then be assigned to tasks.
  3. Adding Tasks
    Click "Add task" on the board, or use the "+" button in any column header (except "Done") to create a task pre-filled with that status. Fill in a title, description, due date, priority (Urgent, Medium, or Low), category, assigned contacts, and optional subtasks, then click "Create Task".
  4. Moving Tasks
    Drag and drop a task card from one column to another to update its status — for example, from "To do" to "In progress" once you start working on it.
  5. Viewing and Editing Tasks
    Click on a task card to open its details, including due date, priority, assigned contacts, and subtasks. From there you can edit or delete the task using the buttons at the bottom of the view.
  6. Using Subtasks
    When creating or editing a task, add subtasks to break it down into smaller steps. In the task detail view, click a subtask to mark it as done or undone — your progress is shown as a bar on the task card.
  7. Searching for Tasks
    Use the "Find Task" search field on the board to quickly filter tasks by title or description within each column.
